About

From investigations to frameworks

I am a CAMS-certified AML/CFT professional and the designated Principal Officer of Graviti Exchange under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002. In that role I am responsible for the exchange's AML/CFT/CPF compliance programme: policy, risk assessment, controls, regulatory reporting and engagement with the Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND).

Before this, I spent five years in operational financial crime compliance. At AML RightSource I led a team of more than 15 analysts across crypto and fiat transaction monitoring, reviewed and approved SAR/STR filings, and worked on reducing false positives through risk-based alert optimisation. Earlier roles at Quinte Financial Technology, American Express, Airtel Payments Bank and GC Web Ventures covered BSA/AML investigations for US clients, chargeback fraud, RBI and FIU-IND compliance, and KYC/CDD/EDD.

That path shapes how I approach governance: a policy is only as good as the alert queue, the screening hit and the STR narrative it produces. I am currently completing an MBA in IT & Fintech at Manipal University Jaipur.

Leadership & Governance

The Principal Officer remit

VDA service providers are reporting entities under India's PMLA. As the designated Principal Officer of one, my responsibilities span six areas.

Policy & governance

  • Developed AML/CFT/CPF and KYC policies aligned with PMLA and FIU-IND requirements.
  • Established procedures for CDD/EDD, PEPs, sanctions, transaction monitoring, STR reporting, the Travel Rule and record retention.
  • Strengthened compliance governance and escalation frameworks.

Risk assessment

  • Developed the enterprise-wide risk assessment (EWRA) and customer risk-rating frameworks.
  • Assess VDA-specific risk: hosted and unhosted wallets, mixers, darknet exposure and on-chain activity.

Regulatory engagement

  • Primary compliance contact for FIU-IND on AML/CFT matters.
  • Coordinate regulatory documentation, examination readiness, regulatory requests and ongoing reporting obligations.

Monitoring & technology

  • Evaluate AML/KYC and blockchain analytics capabilities.
  • Oversee assessment of transaction monitoring, wallet screening and verification controls, and strengthen alert investigation processes.

Audit & assurance

  • Coordinate independent AML/CFT audits and compliance assessments.
  • Support audit documentation, evidence preparation and control reviews.

Training & capability

  • Conceived and developed Graviti AML Academy, an internal employee training initiative.
  • Structured its curriculum on AML/CFT/CPF, VDA compliance and financial crime, with case studies and assessments.

Earlier people leadership: at AML RightSource I led 15+ analysts — case allocation, queue management, high-risk alert prioritisation, QA reviews, KPI management (quality, turnaround, productivity) and analyst mentoring. Descriptions on this site are limited to my own responsibilities; no confidential employer or customer information is published.
